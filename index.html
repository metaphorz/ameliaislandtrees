About the data

Sources describe different things and are clustered separately by color. Counts are records, not a census of unique or currently living trees.

The Fernandina inventory has 14,034 records and layer metadata last edited in March 2023. County records include mainland points. Omni observations cover trees and woody plants, may repeat the same plant, and do not provide tree-condition assessments.

Additional city layers contain 84 designated heritage trees, 743 planting records, and 24 historical nomination assessments. Nomination is not designation. Records within 5 m of a city inventory point have a nearby-record link for review; proximity alone does not prove they describe the same tree.

Names and notes come from recorded source fields. PDF photo sheets open as documents. Latitude and longitude are the source’s public point location in decimal degrees; an obscured observation is approximate.

AIPCA planting locations include planned, reported planted, and unknown-status records. The public invasive-plant layer currently has no records. Planting and observation records with no condition are labeled “Not recorded.”

Fort Clinch shading shows DEP maritime-hammock habitat boundaries, not individual trees or measured canopy cover. The September 2026 GIS snapshot has 54 areas totaling about 804 recorded acres; its download date is not a field-survey date. The layer includes park-managed areas west of Amelia Island.
